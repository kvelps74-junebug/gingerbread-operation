How to Play

Use the tongs to carefully remove each baking item from the gingerbread man.

Avoid touching the edges of the cavity. If the tongs touch an edge, the gingerbread man's nose will buzz and you will lose one ticket.

Once an item is completely removed from its cavity, carry it to the Removed Items tray and release it there to complete the extraction.

Remove the item → Move it to the tray → Release to score
