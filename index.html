How to Play

Grab an item with the tongs and carefully lift it upward out of its cavity.

Keep the item centered while lifting. If you touch the cavity edge, the gingerbread man's nose will buzz and you will lose one gameplay ticket.

Once the item is safely extracted, carry it to the Removed Items Tray and release it there.

Lift carefully → Clear the cavity → Place on tray

Everyone earns 1 prize ticket just for playing!
